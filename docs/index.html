01

Drag it. Drop it. Done.

Drag any file or image toward the right edge of your screen, from anywhere — Finder, a browser, another app. Carpet opens on its own and catches it. No copy, no dialog, no folders to pick.

02

Copy something worth keeping?

Carpet notices and offers to keep it for three seconds. Say yes and it's saved. Say nothing and it's gone — nothing you copy is ever stored without you deciding first.

03

A board, not a folder.

Everything you save lands on a Pinterest-style board — images at their real size, notes and links as colored cards. Scan it at a glance instead of digging through nested folders.

04

Ask your Carpet.

Search by what you remember, not what you typed — "that article about the early Facebook engineer" is enough. Powered by a local AI model running on your machine via Ollama. Nothing is ever sent to the cloud.

100% local. No account. No cloud.

Every file, note, and embedding stays on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Carpet. The optional AI search runs against a local Ollama server on localhost — Carpet never phones home.
